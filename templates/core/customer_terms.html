Customer terms and policies

These terms explain how Kena ticket purchases and accounts work.

1. Accounts

Keep your account details accurate and protect your password and verification codes. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account.

2. Tickets and payment

Ticket prices are shown in Maloti. You must pay the exact amount shown for your order. Manual mobile-money payments remain pending until an authorised organiser or Kena reviewer verifies the transaction.

Never share a mobile-money PIN with Kena, an organiser or anyone claiming to provide support.

3. Online tickets are non-refundable

Online tickets purchased through Kena are non-refundable, including where a customer changes their mind, cannot attend, arrives late or submits an incorrect order. Any event cancellation or postponement will be handled according to the event information and applicable organiser arrangements.

4. Ticket use

Keep your ticket and its QR code secure. A ticket may be checked at entry and may only be admitted according to the event's admission rules. Do not duplicate, alter or misuse tickets.

5. Event information

Events are organised by independent organisers. Check the event page for the venue, time, admission rules and organiser-specific information before paying.

6. Support

Contact kena.support@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp +266 5853 8173 if you need help.
