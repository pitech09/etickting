Organiser terms and policies

The responsibilities of organisers using Kena to publish and manage events.

1. Accurate event information

Organisers must provide complete, accurate and current event details, including venue, dates, ticket prices, capacity, admission rules and any event-specific policies.

2. Ticket inventory and admission

Organisers are responsible for the inventory they publish, the event experience, entry staff and the decision to admit guests. Use Kena's verification tools and do not issue duplicate or unauthorised tickets.

3. Payment verification

Manual mobile-money payments must be checked against the actual provider transaction record before approval. Screenshots and customer claims alone are not sufficient. Organisers must not request customer PINs.

4. Commission and settlement

Kena does not add a platform service fee to the customer ticket total. For successful sales, the configured platform commission is deducted from organiser settlement. Historical sales retain the rate and financial snapshot applied when they were completed.

5. Cancellations and customer care

Online tickets are non-refundable under the customer policy. Organisers must communicate material event changes promptly and work with Kena support on any cancellation or postponement that affects customers.

6. Lawful content and conduct

Do not publish unlawful, fraudulent, unsafe, discriminatory or misleading events. Kena may restrict or remove content and accounts where necessary to protect customers, organisers or the platform.

7. Support

Contact kena.support@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp +266 5853 8173 for organiser support.
